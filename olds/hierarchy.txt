Quick Reference: TRAP Vector Addresses
TRAP #	Vector Address	Handler
0	0x000000A0	HAL_MEM
1	0x000000A4	HAL_IO
2	0x000000A8	HAL_TIMER
3	0x000000AC	HAL_DATA
4	0x000000B0	HAL_CONTEXT
5	0x000000B4	HAL_SYSTEM
6	0x000000B8	HAL_MOUSE (replaces Radio)
7	0x000000BC	HAL_FRAMEBUFFER
8	0x000000C0	HAL_BLOCKDEV

Key point: Rocket 68 automatically handles TRAP exceptions by reading the vector from 0xA0 + (trap_number * 4). You just need to ensure your system.bin is loaded at ROM_BASE so the vector table is at address 0x00000000. No special configuration in Rocket 68 is needed — it natively supports the 68000 vector table mechanism.
